Every review Liffy has run, newest first, across all your repositories.

Failed reviews stay in the list rather than being cleaned up. A failure is a
fact about your setup — usually a credential or a provider — and hiding it
would make a broken configuration look like an idle one.

Each row carries the status, the repository and pull request, and a one-line
summary. For a failed review that summary is the reason it failed.

Opening a review shows its comments, the model used, and the token count.
Reviews still running update themselves; there is nothing to refresh.
