title: Suggesting a feature, or giving feedback

Ideas are welcome, and they go in the same place as bugs: a GitHub issue,
labelled `enhancement`.

Use **Report a problem** at the bottom of this page and pick *a feature idea*.
It opens a prefilled issue under your own account with the label already
applied, so it lands where the roadmap is kept rather than in a mailbox.

Worth including: what you were trying to do, what you did instead, and why the
current behaviour gets in the way. A suggestion that starts from the problem is
much easier to act on than one that starts from the solution.

Feedback about a *review* — a comment that was wrong or unhelpful — belongs on
the comment itself instead. Thumbs it down: that feeds your Analytics page
directly and needs no issue.
