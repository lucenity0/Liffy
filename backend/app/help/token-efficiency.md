Mean approval rate per 1,000 tokens: how much *useful* review you got for what
it cost to produce.

There is no target, and the trend beside it is the point. A single reading says
almost nothing — the number moves with the size of the diffs you happen to have
opened. What is worth watching is the shape over time, especially after
changing model or provider.

It needs a review with both a token count and at least one rating, so it stays
blank longer than the other tiles.

Token counts include cached tokens. On the subscription providers most of the
volume lands in the cache fields, and counting only fresh input and output
would under-report a review by an order of magnitude — which would make this
figure look far better than the work actually cost.
